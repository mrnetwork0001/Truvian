01 · Desks

One engine. A desk per decision.

Each desk reads the chain itself, buys what it cannot read through Telegraph, applies fixed rules and signs the result. Five free runs a day per IP, no wallet needed.

Token preflight

A trading agent asks: may I buy this token through this pool? Shield buys a security review, total supply, a wash-trading check and pool depth, and checks the supply against its own chain read.

D-03 Mission 13

Holdings proof

An investor checks what a fund or index says a custody wallet holds, without trusting the issuer. Every claim is read on chain at one block and compared with bought balances and supply; an overstated claim is rejected with the exact shortfall.

D-04 New · Solana

Settlement assurance

A custodian is about to pay a counterparty in a Solana token. Shield screens the counterparty, looks it up in its registry, checks the payer's funds, and reads what the token's issuer can still do: freeze it, claw it back through a permanent delegate, tax it, hook it. On RELEASE it pays on devnet and confirms the exact amount arrived at finalized.

Prediction-market resolution

Before a market settles, several ranked miners from different operators are asked for the event's outcome. An answer counts only with a source that names the event; answers are compared with each other and with the market's own result. Every memo's digest is written to Solana devnet.

05 · For agents

One POST, one signed decision.

Same endpoint for every desk; mode picks the desk. Add ?stream=1 for newline-delimited events as each answer lands. Five free runs per IP a day (reset 00:00 UTC), then $0.05 in test USDC on Base Sepolia over x402: one EIP-3009 signature, settled only after the work succeeded.

06 · Limits

What is not true yet.

  1. Testnet money

    Telegraph answers are paid in test USDC from an Anjoman escrow on Ethereum Sepolia; Shield's own charge is test USDC on Base Sepolia; settlements are sent on Solana devnet in test tokens. Token facts are read from mainnets.

  2. Security reviews time out

    Since 22 Nov every routed SECURITY_REVIEW ask has ended in a gateway timeout, so token preflights and listing reviews usually end in REVIEW: nothing is cleared on a missing answer.

  3. Solana balances

    On 25 Nov no miner on the node answered a Solana wallet balance. Ours now does; until the node routes those questions to it, the settlement desk's funds check rests on its own two-provider chain read.

  4. Prediction markets

    Resolution reads Polymarket and Kalshi markets; a Solana-native market API (DFlow) refused our requests, so the Solana part is the memo anchor. Few miners on the node resolve free-text events: of five settled markets tried on 25 Nov, one reached RESOLVED, one DISPUTED, three REVIEW.

  5. Season I check

    The Season I pre-sign check is still online at /app; it is looser than the desks and keeps its Season I wording.
