05 · Prediction-market resolution

Settle on evidence, not one oracle.

Truvian Resolve reads the market from its venue, then buys its outcome from up to three independent miners serving EVENT_OUTCOME_RESOLUTION_GENERAL: the node's routed pick and up to two specialists pinned by id, one per operator, never a miner that reads the same venue. An answer counts only with a source on the event. RESOLVED needs two that agree, and agree with the venue's settlement; sourced answers that disagree are DISPUTED; fewer than two is REVIEW; a market still open is TOO EARLY and buys nothing. The memo is signed, and its digest is written to Solana devnet with the Memo program.

Try one

Telegraph Season II settles on testnet: each answer is $0.01 in test USDC from Shield's Anjoman escrow on Ethereum Sepolia. Market data is read from the venue's free public API. Same free allowance as the token desks (5 a day per IP), then x402.
