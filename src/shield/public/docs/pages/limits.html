Known limitations

  • Security reviews time out. Since 22 Nov 2026 routed SECURITY_REVIEW_GENERAL asks have mostly ended in a gateway timeout, so token preflights and listing reviews usually end in REVIEW.
  • Solana balances. No miner on the node answered a Solana wallet balance on 25 Nov. Truvian's miner now can, but the node routes those questions to it only after its registration is updated.
  • Few resolvers. Few miners resolve free-text events; most markets end in REVIEW.
  • Scores move. Miner scores change every epoch; the leaderboard, not this site, is the record.
  • Season I check. /app is the Season I pre-sign check, looser than the desks: it can say SAFE with checks unanswered.

Keys and your data

There are no accounts. Shield stores what each decision needs to be re-checked: the input you sent, the reads, the answers and the memo, in the latest 100 memos on the server. It keeps a count of free runs per IP address for the day. Payments are signed in your own wallet; Shield never sees a private key. Shield's own keys live only in the server's environment.

FAQ

Why so many REVIEWs?

Because Truvian never clears on a missing or unchecked answer. A REVIEW says which check could not be made and why; that is the product working, not failing.

Can a miner game a decision?

One answer cannot clear anything on its own: supply and balances are checked against Shield's chain reads, an answer about another token or address is set aside, and a second opinion can hold a decision back but never clear it.

What does a decision cost?

About $0.01 per answer, $0.02 to $0.06 per decision in test USDC, shown in each memo's cost line. A fact bought in the last 10 minutes is reused at $0.

Is any of this mocked?

No answer is mocked. The trade is simulated and the money is testnet, as the table above says; everything else is a live read or a live, paid answer.

How do I check a memo myself?

Download it from its page, compute SHA-256 over its canonical JSON, and recover the EIP-191 signer of Truvian Shield memo sha256:<digest>. It must be 0x956aECB0b0DEfC2f2631C26ec0FF7366Ebb53245.

Is Truvian part of Telegraph?

No. It is an independent entry in Telegraph Protocol's Season II hackathon that uses Telegraph as its source of specialist answers.
