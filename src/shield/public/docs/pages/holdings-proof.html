Holdings proof.

An investor checks what a fund or index says a custody wallet holds, without trusting the issuer, and gets VERIFIED, REVIEW or REJECT. This is Telegraph Mission 13 (Asset Management). It works on EVM chains and, since 25 Nov 2026, on Solana.

How to use it

Open /preflight?desk=holdings or call POST /api/preflight with mode: "holdings".

FieldWhat it isNotes
chainan EVM chain, or solana / solana-devnetSolana takes base58 addresses
walletthe custody wallet the fund namesrequired
holdings[{token, claimed, pool?}], at most 3claimed in whole tokens; pool (EVM only) is the pool the holding is rebalanced through

The rules

FindingSeverityWhen
holding.shortblockthe chain shows less than claimed, beyond 10 bps; the memo states the exact shortfall
holding.not_a_contractblockthe claimed token is not a contract (or not a mint on Solana)
balance.conflictreviewTelegraph's balance is more than 50 bps from the chain read
balance.not_about_walletreviewthe answer gives a balance for another address
mint.permanent_delegate (Solana)reviewthe issuer can move or burn tokens out of the holding
holding.float_shareinfothe holding's share of the token's supply, for rebalancing

VERIFIED only when every claim is covered on the chain and every bought answer (balance, supply and, with a pool, liquidity) agrees with it.

How it is built

runHoldings in src/shield/holdings.ts reads every holding at one block (readHoldings) or, on Solana, at one finalized slot (readSolanaHoldings in src/shield/solanaHoldings.ts), then evaluateHoldings applies claimFindings, balanceFindings and the shared supply and liquidity rules. Solana balances sum every account the wallet holds of the mint, listed on one provider and re-read by address on a second (readBalance in src/shield/solana.ts).
