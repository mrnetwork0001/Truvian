Building on Telegraph mainnet.

Truvian is built to be a paid decision service on Telegraph mainnet, not a hackathon demo: these are the plans, in order, and only the first phase is shipped. Status chips are honest: Live is running today, In progress is being built or waiting on one step, Planned is not started. Phases have no dates except the end of Season II, 1 Dec 2026; mainnet timing depends on Telegraph.

Now: Season II on testnet Live

  • Five desks: token preflight, listing review, holdings proof (EVM and Solana), settlement assurance on Solana, market resolution. Live
  • Miner 6194 answering six onchain intents on 14 EVM chains, and Solana in its own API. Live
  • Champion evaluators 187 (SMART_CONTRACT_AUDIT) and 242 (CORPORATE_REGISTRY_LOOKUP). Live
  • Pay per run over x402 in test USDC after 5 free runs a day, signed in the visitor's wallet. Live
  • Solana devnet settlement with read-back, and devnet anchoring of resolution memos. Live

Next: close the gaps the testnet showed In progress

  • Register the miner description that includes Solana, so the node routes Solana balance and transfer questions to our miner: done 25 Nov, registration 6194; it is ranked after its 7-day grace period. Live
  • More evaluator adapters for the intents the desks buy, and second versions of the ones that lost. In progress
  • Fewer REVIEWs: when the routed security review times out, ask a second source instead of waiting on the same route. Planned
  • A Solana-native prediction-market source for the resolution desk (the one tried refused requests). Planned

Mainnet launch: real money, with limits Planned

  • Real-USDC payments for runs over x402 on Base mainnet, and an escrow funded with real USDC for Telegraph answers. Planned
  • Solana mainnet settlement behind a per-customer policy: allowed tokens and counterparties, a cap per payment and per day, and a person's approval above it. Planned
  • Memo anchoring on mainnet, so a decision's existence is provable on a chain that persists. Planned
  • Uptime monitoring and alerts on every route, the miner and the evaluator executors. Planned
  • API keys with per-key rate limits, replacing the per-IP free allowance for paying customers. Planned

After: a product teams run every day Planned

  • A usage dashboard: decisions, spend, and every memo per key, with audit export. Planned
  • Webhooks: a decision delivered to the customer's system the moment it is signed. Planned
  • Per-desk policies: each customer sets its own thresholds (supply tolerance, liquidity floor, which issuer powers block). Planned
  • Continuous monitoring: holdings re-proved on a schedule, markets watched until they settle, alerts when a decision would change. Planned
  • Volume pricing: pay per decision, cheaper per decision at volume. Planned

Every paid decision buys answers from Telegraph miners, so Truvian's growth is paid intelligence demand on Telegraph: the more decisions customers buy, the more the network's ranked specialists earn.
